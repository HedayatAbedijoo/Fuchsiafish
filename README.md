# Fuchsiafish

Fuchsiafish are fictional creatures from the world of the novel **ACE.await**. They feed on our data, learn from our lives, and drain our phone batteries. They help Blueshark grow smarter, but their insatiable appetite consumes energy and threatens to plunge cities into darkness.

Smartphones are the Fuchsiafish's main target: they need to get inside them to feed on our data and drain their power. If smartphones go dark, a simple keypad phone could help us stay connected. This campaign imagines that threat and asks us to keep a non-smartphone as a backup.

## The novel ACE.await

**ACE.await** is a literary speculative novel about the choices we make and the world our creations inherit.

Hedayat is an Iranian immigrant and programmer living in Cologne, Germany. Struggling with the consequences of his past decisions, he accepts a stranger’s invitation to play ACE: Agency, Connection, and Exchange. The game is designed to help people make more conscious decisions. As it unfolds, three characters challenge Hedayat with difficult choices.

First, Mr. Writer—the protagonist of Hedayat’s unfinished novel—steps out of its pages. He offers Hedayat a way to end the cycle of stories nested within one another.

Next, Adam, Hedayat’s digital child, uncovers the secret of 2313 and returns with a strange request about love.

Finally, an old friend holds the truth about Hedayat’s digital grandchildren and the origins of the Fuchsiafish. These creatures learned greed from humans and consume energy at an alarming rate.

Through these characters and events, the novel explores consciousness, agency, responsibility, and what creators owe their creations.
